# NECB 2026 — Invitation Letter Template

Reusable template for international poster or talk presenters who need an
official letter of invitation to support a visa application or administrative
procedures at their home institution. Personalize the fields marked `{{ }}`.

Print on Broad Institute / MGH / Harvard Medical School letterhead (whichever
co-chair is signing), or use the plain-text form below on New England
Computational Biology Symposium letterhead if we prepare one.

---

**Subject:** NECB 2026 · Letter of Invitation — {{presenter_name}}

Dear {{presenter_name}},

On behalf of the New England Computational Biology Symposium (NECB) 2026
Organizing Committee, I am pleased to confirm that your abstract,

> **"{{abstract_title}}"**  (abstract ID: {{abstract_id}})

has been accepted for presentation as a {{poster | selected talk}} at NECB 2026,
which will be held **October 1–2, 2026** at **Microsoft Research New England,
One Memorial Drive, Cambridge, Massachusetts 02142, United States**.

NECB 2026 is a two-day symposium bringing together researchers in computational
biology, machine learning, and the life sciences from academic institutions,
research hospitals, and industry across New England and beyond. The symposium
is organized in partnership with the International Society for Computational
Biology (ISCB) and features keynote lectures, contributed talks, and poster
sessions.

Your participation in the symposium as a presenter is by invitation of the
Organizing Committee. We look forward to your contribution.

Please note that NECB 2026 does not provide funding for travel, accommodation,
or visa fees; all such expenses are the responsibility of the participant or
their home institution. This letter is issued solely to support your visa
application and administrative procedures.

Should the United States embassy or consulate require further verification,
they may contact us at newenglandcompbio@gmail.com.

Sincerely,

**Ignacio Vazquez-Garcia, Ph.D.**
Co-Chair, NECB 2026 Organizing Committee
Assistant Investigator, Massachusetts General Hospital
Assistant Professor, Harvard Medical School
Associate Member, Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard
newenglandcompbio@gmail.com · https://newenglandcompbio.org
Tel: {{signatory_phone}}   ← required by some universities (e.g. HKU) for travel approval
