## Presentation format

- **10 minutes** presentation + **5 minutes** Q&A. Please time to 10 min;
  a moderator will signal when you have 1 min remaining.
- We'll assign your specific slot within the session and share the final
  program by **Fri Sep 18**.

## Presentation laptop

- **Talks will run from a single venue laptop (Windows PC)** — no
  bring-your-own-device, and no switching machines between speakers.
- **Please load your slides onto the venue laptop the morning of the day
  you present.** We'll have someone at the AV table before the first
  session on both days to help. Details on the file drop-off will follow.
- **File format:** PowerPoint (`.pptx`) is the primary format. PDF works
  as a backup. Please avoid Keynote or Google Slides exports unless
  you've tested the resulting file on a Windows machine — embedded fonts
  and animations sometimes render incorrectly.
- **Fonts, videos, and animations:** embed any custom fonts in your
  `.pptx` (File → Options → Save → *Embed fonts in the file*). Test any
  video clips ahead of time, and bring the source `.mp4` alongside your
  slides in case the embedded copy misbehaves.
- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 widescreen. **[to confirm with Kevin]**

## Files & version control

- Please **bring your slides on a USB stick** as a fallback in case the
  cloud upload has issues on the day.
- If you need to update your slides between sessions, please do so at
  the AV table between talks, not during your slot.
