MIT FutureFest Salon

Held on Thursday, October 1, as the evening program of NECB 2026. Thank you to everyone who joined the conversation.

Topic

Mind, Body and Soul
Frontiers of Science, Health, and Intelligence

What does artificial intelligence mean for the human mind? How will it transform the human body and human health, what does it tell us about the human soul, and what does it mean for humanity as a whole? A joint salon of MIT FutureFest, NECB and the MIT/Harvard conversation salon series, bringing together scientists, clinicians, technologists and thinkers for an evening of serious conversation and playful debate.

When

Thursday, October 1, 2026
6:00 – 9:00 PM

Where

Microsoft Research New England
One Memorial Drive, Cambridge, MA 02142

Format

Table discussions by theme, each between two opposing answers, followed by a whole-room conversation and refreshments.

Featured speakers

Manolis Kellis (MIT) · Smita Krishnaswamy (Yale) · Tomaso Poggio (MIT) · Jim DiCarlo (MIT) · Donna Slonim (Tufts)

The evening's questions

Sixteen tables each argued one question between two opposing answers, then brought their conclusions back to the whole room.

Mind
Consciousness · Super-intelligence · Prediction vs. understanding
Body
Health · Biology · AI science · Enhancement and longevity
Soul
Mathematics and discovery · Art · Curation · Truth
Humanity
Society · Careers · Education · Safety and control · Arc of humanity
