MIT FutureFest Salon

Joint evening event with MIT FutureFest on Day 1 of NECB 2026 — same venue, immediately after the closing keynote.

Topic

Mind, Body and Soul
Frontiers of Science, Health, and Intelligence

What does artificial intelligence mean for the human mind? How will it transform the human body and human health, what does it tell us about the human soul, and what does it mean for humanity as a whole? A joint salon of MIT FutureFest, NECB and the MIT/Harvard conversation salon series, bringing together scientists, clinicians, technologists and thinkers for an evening of serious conversation and playful debate.
